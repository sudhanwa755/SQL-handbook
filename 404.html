404 NOT FOUND

The requested URL produced an empty result set. The tuple you are seeking does not exist in our relation schema.

exception-handler.sql 
SELECT page_id, title, url 
FROM handbook_registry 
WHERE url = '/requested-page';

-- Diagnostic Output:
-- [404] ORA-01403: no data found in schema
-- Execution completed in 0.002s (0 tuples returned)
