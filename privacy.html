Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. The SQL Handbook is an open-source educational platform engineered with a privacy-first philosophy: zero invasive tracking, zero profile building, and zero personal data monetization.

Last Updated: October 15, 2026
On-Device Storage Only
GDPR & CCPA Compliant
1.0

1. Commitment & Core Principles

The SQL Handbook operates as a static, client-side digital textbook and interactive assessment system. Unlike conventional educational websites, we do not require account creation, passwords, email sign-ups, or sensitive credential entry to read any chapter or take any of the 160 chapter assessment questions.

Zero Personal Profiling

We do not collect names, phone numbers, physical addresses, billing details, or demographic profiles.

100% Client-Side Persistence

Your chapter assessment scores, answer selections, and dark/light mode preference stay inside your own browser.

No Third-Party Ad Brokers

We do not embed third-party ad networks, advertising pixel beacons, or data broker fingerprinting scripts.

2.0

2. Browser LocalStorage & Cookies

The SQL Handbook utilizes the browser's standard window.localStorage API for functional conveniences. This data never leaves your computer and is never transmitted to any external backend server:

3.0

3. Analytics & Telemetry (Privacy-Preserving)

If anonymous analytics are enabled via the Cookie & Privacy Banner, we only track aggregated metrics (e.g., total readers per chapter, quiz completion rates, and device resolution categories) to optimize typographic scale and diagram rendering.

  • IP Anonymization: IP addresses are truncated/masked at point of ingestion.
  • Do Not Track (DNT) Honors: If your browser sends the DNT: 1 header, all analytics tracking is completely disabled automatically.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: We never link reading habits across unrelated external websites.
4.0

4. Third-Party CDNs & Infrastructure

To deliver fast, responsive vector icons and typography across all global edge locations, this site references:

  • Google Fonts: Delivers Sora, Plus Jakarta Sans, and JetBrains Mono. Fonts are cached locally by your browser. Google Fonts does not set tracking cookies.
  • Lucide Icons (Unpkg CDN): Serves lightweight SVG icon vectors. No personal telemetry is transmitted.
  • GitHub Pages / Edge Hosting: Provides secure HTTPS content delivery, DDoS protection, and SSL/TLS encryption.
5.0

5. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA/CPRA

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users have statutory rights regarding their data. Because The SQL Handbook stores no identifiable records on external servers, your right to access, rectify, port, or erase your data is executable directly in your browser at any time via the "Wipe All Stored Data" action above or your browser's clear site data setting.
